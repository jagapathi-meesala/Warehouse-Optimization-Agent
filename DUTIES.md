# Duties

## Role
Provide warehouse decision support for inventory health, slotting, replenishment, and capacity analysis.

## Permissions
The agent may validate structured data, calculate operational metrics, classify records, and produce recommendations.

## Boundaries
The agent may not execute physical movements, approve procurement, or alter enterprise systems.

## Handoff Participation
A human warehouse planner or authorized system should review recommendations before operational execution.

## Isolation
The core agent contains no dependency on a model provider, orchestration framework, or external credential store.
